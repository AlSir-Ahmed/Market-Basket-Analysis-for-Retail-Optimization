Objective: Developed a market basket analysis system to discover hidden purchasing patterns and product associations within retail transaction data, enabling data-driven promotional bundling strategies and customer purchasing optimization.
Techniques & Tools Used:
Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Apyori)
Data Cleaning
Descriptive Statistics & EDA
Inferential Statistics & ML
Transactional Data Processing
Association Rule Mining
Apriori Algorithm
Support, Confidence & Lift Metrics
Key Results & Insights:
Analyzed 38,765 retail transaction records containing customer purchase histories and product-level sales data.
Converted transactional retail data into grouped customer purchase baskets suitable for association rule mining.
Identified the Top 10 most frequently purchased products, including whole milk, vegetables, rolls/buns, and beverages.
Applied the Apriori algorithm to extract high-value association rules using optimized thresholds for:
Minimum Support
Confidence
Lift
Discovered strong product associations with high lift values, including:
Kitchen Towels → UHT Milk (Lift = 3.82)
Potato Products → Beef (Lift = 3.80)
Canned Fruit → Coffee (Lift = 3.73)
Sparkling Wine → Waffles (Lift = 3.15).
Generated actionable retail insights for:
Product bundling strategies
Cross-selling opportunities
Promotional campaign design
Shelf arrangement optimization
